    How It Works

    Transparent formulas, scientific references and calculation assumptions.

    Producing plastic from petrochemicals is energy intensive. Recycling recovers the polymer and skips most of that step. RecycleMeter estimates the difference.

    1. Tell us what you recycled

      Pick the plastic type and enter the weight, in kilograms or pounds. Pounds are converted at 1 lb = {{ registry.units.kg_per_lb }} kg.

    2. We apply the emission factor

      Each kilogram is multiplied by the emissions saved when recycled material replaces virgin plastic.

    3. We translate it

      The total is expressed as trees, litres of petrol and smartphone charges, so it is easier to picture.

    The formula

    CO₂ saved (kg)= Weight recycled (kg)× (EFvirgin − EFrecycled)

    EF is an emission factor in kg CO₂e per kg of plastic. The difference is the net saving per kilogram, shown in the right-hand column below. For a monthly figure, the result is also multiplied by 12 to give an annual total.

    Methodology & Science

    System Scope & Recycling Methodology

    All carbon displacement values and emission factors in RecycleMeter are modeled strictly on closed-loop mechanical recycling (collection, sorting, shredding, hot washing, and extrusion/compression molding). Unlike thermal chemical recycling (such as pyrolysis or gasification), mechanical recycling largely preserves the base polymer chains using physical force and moderate electrical heat, avoiding the massive energy footprint of fossil petrochemical cracking. Calculations reflect displacement of primary virgin resin pellets, following the principles of ISO 14044/14067 and informed by the US EPA WARM dataset (Franklin Associates & APR).

    Calculation assumptions

    • The weight entered is plastic that was actually recycled, not only collected.
    • Each kilogram of recycled plastic replaces one kilogram of virgin plastic of the same type.
    • Emission factors are averages for mechanical recycling. Real values vary with the facility, energy source, transport and material quality.
    • Mixed plastics use a composite saving of {{ registry.materials[3].multiplier }} kg CO₂e per kg.
    • Equivalents are illustrative. They do not mean trees were planted or fuel was saved.
    • Results are indicative estimates, not a certified carbon footprint.

    About

    About RecycleMeter

    RecycleMeter is a research-led sustainability initiative developed under the scientific leadership of Prof. Suryasarathi Bose, Department of Materials Engineering, Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru.

    RecycleMeter is a non-profit, open-access sustainability tool developed to foster climate literacy across schools, universities, and residential communities. This platform operates without commercial interest or advertising to provide transparent environmental accounting grounded in peer-reviewed research.

    The platform aims to make scientific methods for assessing the environmental impact of plastic recycling accessible to individuals, educational institutions, researchers and organisations.

    Using established methodologies, including the US Environmental Protection Agency's Waste Reduction Model (EPA WARM), RecycleMeter helps users understand the potential greenhouse gas benefits associated with recycling different types of plastics.

    The initiative seeks to bridge scientific research and public awareness by translating complex environmental calculations into simple, transparent and accessible information.
